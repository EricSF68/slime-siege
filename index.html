How to Play

Goal

Slimes ooze in from the right. Build robots on the 5×9 grid to stop them before they reach your base on the left. Survive every wave to win the level.

Energy

Robots cost energy. Tap the glowing cells that drop from above (+25 each). Sparkplugs make more energy over time, so build a few early. Turn on Auto-collect energy in Settings if you'd rather not tap.

Building robots

Tap a robot card at the top, then tap a square. Or drag the card onto the square. A card shows its cost; it greys out while recharging or when you can't afford it. Use Remove (the wrench) to clear a square.

Fusion

Place a robot on top of another to fuse them into something stronger. Squares that glow gold can take the selected robot; a padlock means that fusion isn't unlocked yet. Fusions keep going: a Blaster on a Twin Blaster makes a Gatling Bot, and three tier-3 fusions unlock after Level 9. A Bulwark placed on a damaged wall repairs it. All 23 recipes are in the Almanac.

Slimes

Each slime eats the first robot in its way. Some wear armor, some carry shields that block straight shots (lob or zap over them), Hoppers jump your first robot, Spitters shoot from range, and Hotheads charge once their pot breaks. Check the Almanac for each one's trick.

Grabber and Tractor Beam

Grabber (unlocks on Level 4): tap a robot to lift it, then tap an empty square to move it, or another robot to fuse the two. It recharges for 10 seconds. Tractor Beam (Level 6): tap a slime to drag it into the next lane. It recharges for 40 seconds.

Loadout

Bring up to 10 cards. Tap a robot once to add it, again to add a second copy (it costs double but has its own recharge), and a third time to remove it. The Supply Crate opens into a random robot, sometimes a fusion.

Scrap caps, scorch and night shifts

When a slime's metal cap breaks it drops on the floor. Tap it, then tap a basic (unfused) robot to bolt it on as armor. Big bombs leave a scorched square you can't build on for about 20 seconds. On Night Shift levels less energy falls and scrap crates block some squares; clear them with Remove.

Sweepers and losing

Each lane has one Sweeper drone at the base. The first slime to get through sets it off and clears the lane. If another slime gets through that lane after that, the slimes win.

Controls

Touch or mouse: tap cards, squares and energy. ⏸ pauses; the speed button cycles 1×, 2× and ½×. Tap during the countdown to skip it.
Keyboard: 1–9, 0 pick a robot · W remove · G grab · T beam · F speed · Space pause · Esc cancel.

Tips

Two Sparkplugs per lane is a strong start. Put walls (Bulwark) in front of your shooters. Save a Bomb Bot for big slimes. Watch the wave bar: flags mark the big surges.
