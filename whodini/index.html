How to Play

Goal

Candidates stream in from the lobby on the right. Place recruiters on the 5×9 floor to win them over before they walk out the competitor exit on the left. Fill the req by getting through every round.

Leads

Recruiters cost Leads. Tap the purple sparkle coins that drift down (+25 each). Sourcerers conjure more Leads over time, so place a few early. Turn on Auto-collect Leads in Settings if you'd rather not tap.

Placing recruiters

Tap a card at the top, then tap a square, or drag the card onto the square. Screeners fire questions down their lane; Panel Interviews hold candidates in place. Use Remove to clear a square.

Whodini AI features

AI features go on top of a recruiter and make them stronger. Hamilton is the AI copilot: Screeners ask twice as many questions, Sourcerers double their Leads. Apoachability scores who is ready to move: Screeners' questions pierce the whole lane. Squares that glow gold can take the selected card. All 19 upgrades are in the Almanac.

Candidates

Each candidate burns out the first recruiter in their way. Passive Candidates wear headphones, Ghosters hide behind a Do Not Disturb sign (toss cards over it), Job Hoppers skip your first recruiter, Skeptics object from range, and Flight Risks bolt when their suitcase drops.

Grabber and Reassign

Grabber (Level 4): tap a recruiter to lift them, then tap an empty square to move them, or another recruiter to combine. Recharges in 10 seconds. Reassign (Level 6): tap a candidate to move them to the next lane. Recharges in 40 seconds.

Loadout

Bring up to 10 cards. Tap a card once to add it, again to add a second copy (double cost, its own recharge), and a third time to remove it. The Talent Pool opens into a random recruiter, sometimes an AI-upgraded one.

Headphones, coffee and After Hours

When a Passive Candidate's headphones come off they drop on the floor. Tap them, then tap a basic recruiter to give them focus (extra armor). A Job Fair leaves spilled coffee you can't build on for about 20 seconds. In After Hours rounds fewer Leads fall and paperwork blocks some squares; clear it with Remove.

Exploding Offers and losing

Each lane has one Exploding Offer cart by the exit. The first candidate to slip through sets it off and it clears the lane. If another candidate gets through that lane after that, you lose them to a competitor.

Controls

Touch or mouse: tap cards, squares and Leads. ⏸ pauses; the speed button cycles 1×, 2× and ½×. Tap during the countdown to skip it.
Keyboard: 1–9, 0 pick a card · W remove · G grab · T reassign · F speed · Space pause · Esc cancel.

Tips

Two Sourcerers per lane is a strong start. Put a Panel Interview in front of your Screeners. Hamilton on a Sourcerer early pays for itself. Save a Job Fair for Executives. Flags on the round bar mark the big surges.
